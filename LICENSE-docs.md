## What this covers

`src/video/uvcdec/turbo/`: `README.md`, `NOTICE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/`
(the design notes, the test records, the status and the release notes) and
the `README.md` files beside the drivers and their test data.

Everything else is unchanged:

  `GPL-2.0-or-later` ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).

  ([NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

  under its own licences ([src/video/uvcdec/turbo/LICENSE.md](src/video/uvcdec/turbo/LICENSE.md)).
