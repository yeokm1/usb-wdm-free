## Findings for the design (Owner, 2026-10-22: accepted, recorded in the design)

- 5.4: in master the LED-on and auth-done packets go to every GIP pad
  (v6.12: PDP only); ANNOUNCE only starts init for `FLAG_DELAY_INIT` pads
  (one: `366C:0005`). Byte 2 of every init packet is overwritten with the
  sequence number, including the Elite 2 `4d 10 01 ...` packet.
- 5.1: keyboards/keypads with an XInput function (Wooting, Azeron) are left
  out of the INFs; the driver also fails a start when any interface is not
  vendor class 0xFF, so a forced composite keyboard keeps nothing broken.
- 6.2: with `GuideButton` 0, the trigger buttons become 11 and 12
  (contiguous), not 12 and 13.
- 8: the 9x INF does not copy `hidclass.sys`/`hidparse.sys` (M1 question).
- The receiver list from master has three ids: `045E:0291`, `045E:02A9`,
  `045E:0719` (98 SE/ME INF only).
