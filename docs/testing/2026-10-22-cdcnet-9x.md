# CDCNET on Windows 98 SE under NUSB's EHCI and xHCI98, 2026-10-22

The 98 SE legs the owner's rule asks for (`AGENTS.md`: every driver's
legs under a USB 2.0 EHCI host controller driver, NUSB's on 98 SE, and
under xHCI98; 98 SE is the gate). Before this, `cdcnet` had run on 98 SE
only on Windows' own UHCI driver ([2026-10-22-cdcnet.md](2026-10-22-cdcnet.md)).
Every leg here is QEMU (TCG) with the `usb-net` model: a VM pass, never a
"Tested" cell. Nothing is released.

## What made an EHCI leg possible: a High Speed `usb-net`

QEMU's `usb-net` is Full Speed only, so it cannot be an EHCI leg (the
earlier record's "Not run here"). [`qemu-usbnet-hs.patch`](qemu-usbnet-hs.patch)
(test-only, QEMU 11.1.0 `hw/usb/dev-network.c`) adds `highspeed=on`: a
High Speed descriptor set (bulk 512 bytes, interrupt `bInterval` 9) beside
the Full Speed one, and the ECM short-packet rule taken from the
endpoint's own `wMaxPacketSize` instead of a fixed 64. The device then
attaches at 480 Mb/s on `usb-ehci` and on `qemu-xhci`'s USB 2 ports; the
driver's trace confirms it ("pipes in 512 out 512", "high speed").

The patched QEMU is built with `--disable-slirp` (the MSYS2 build has no
libslirp), so the guest's netdev is a `dgram` socket on 127.0.0.1, joined
on a hub to a `-netdev user` in a second, stock QEMU 11.1.0 started with
`-machine none` (no guest, no vCPU work): DHCP and TFTP still come from
QEMU's own slirp, and no host program serves the guest.

## Setup

Kit `E:\usb-wdm-free\net9x\` (scripts under `scripts\`, ports
48700-48719): headless `-machine pc -accel tcg -cpu pentium3,-apic -smp 1
-m 256`, `-action reboot=shutdown` (cold starts only: QEMU's warm reset
wedges 98), overlays on:

| Image | Contents |
|---|---|
| `w98-hcd310` | 98 SE with xHCI98 3.1.0.0 installed (`qemu-xhci,p2=4,p3=4`) |
| `w98-nusb` | the same plus NUSB 3.3; the device on `usb-ehci` runs on NUSB's EHCI driver |

`cdcnet.sys` is the ndis5 **trace** build (writes to port 0xE9), copied
over `C:\WINDOWS\SYSTEM\cdcnet.sys` from the floppy before the device is
hot-plugged, after a first install from `CDCT98.INF`
(`c1d51c4d0f47890268e12540036f49a52e3c75b9048dcae20352c34ffcfc6481`).
`NETTEST.EXE` (`501a9f54...424a`, as before): DHCP, 21 ICMP echoes at the
seven frame sizes, then N x 1 MiB TFTP downloads, every byte checked;
N comes from `A:\NETTEST.CFG`.

**Removal under traffic** (`remloop.ps1`, per cycle): NETTEST starts,
`device_del` 40 s later, `device_add` 15 s after that, then the cycle
checks the trace for a fresh "initialized" and keeps the NETTEST log.

Host load: CPU about 97 %, 13+ QEMU guests from other work; the timings
below are noisy and only compare runs taken back to back.

## Driver fixes these legs found

1. **`2e6b56a`** (xHCI98, Full Speed): a removal under traffic hung the
   guest 3 times in 7 when a control request was the first to see the
   device gone: the 6 data transfers in flight were never completed and
   NDIS's unbind waited for them. The worker now drains every transfer
   before it reports the device gone. After it: 6 of 6 cycles pass.
2. **`8438c07`** (NUSB EHCI): at every removal (4 of 4) NDIS called the
   set handler with `OID_PNP_SET_POWER` (`fd010101`) **after** `Halt` had
   freed the adapter. In the first run (`e1d`) the write into freed pool
   was followed, during traffic on the replugged adapter, by "A fatal
   exception 0E has occurred at 0028:FF0A23A8", and the next shutdown's
   halt hung in an abort-pipe request ("sync: URB 2 timed out"); the
   guest was quit. `Query` and `Set` now check their context against a
   table of live adapters (added when `Initialize` succeeds, dropped
   when `Halt` begins) and refuse a halted one without touching it
   (trace "set oid fd010101 on a halted adapter: refused"). Not seen
   under xHCI98 or UHCI.

## Legs, ECM (configuration 1)

Binary for the post-fix runs (`e2`, `x1i`, `x1j`): ndis5 trace
`fc1dbb00ffa413735dca0fd80c08a865fc712fa55aa1b28065d96afec685cac7`
(source `8438c07`).

### 98 SE, NUSB 3.3 EHCI, High Speed

- `e1c` (before `8438c07`): NETTEST 25 x 1 MiB, 21/21 ICMP, every round
  verified, no retransmits (19.6-30.8 s per MiB). **PASS.** Shutdown halt
  clean.
- `e1d` (before `8438c07`): removal under traffic; the replug
  re-initialized and NETTEST finished, but with the 0E fault above.
  **FAIL**, fixed by `8438c07`.
- `e2` (after): 3 removal cycles under traffic (6 x 1 MiB each, cut in
  round 2): each re-initialized at High Speed; the running NETTEST
  recovered on the new adapter (cycles 1, 2: round 2 bridged by TFTP
  retransmits, all 6 verified; cycle 3: round 2 timed out, rounds 3-6
  verified). Each removal: "fault c000009d", "device gone: drained",
  halt, the stale set refused. Shutdown halt clean ("halted: tx 8237/0").
  **PASS.**

### 98 SE, xHCI98 3.1.0.0 (`qemu-xhci`)

- Full Speed (stock QEMU `usb-net`, `x1`-`x1g`): NETTEST 2 x 1 MiB
  **PASS**; removal under traffic 6 of 6 cycles after `2e6b56a` **PASS**
  (fix 1 above).
- High Speed, first build of the QEMU patch (`x1h`): "start failed at
  stage 5, status c000000d" (the data interface's alternate setting).
  With the final build (the patch as committed) it did not recur. The
  first build did not register `usb_desc_attach`, so the High Speed set
  was not chosen at attach (on EHCI that build's device came up with
  64-byte pipes, `e1b`); that is the likely cause, not proven.
- High Speed (`x1i`, after `8438c07`): 3 cycles of NETTEST 6 x 1 MiB
  (2.7-3.7 s per MiB), removal and replug: all re-initialized at High
  Speed, all PASS. These removals came after NETTEST had finished (idle).
- High Speed under traffic (`x1j`): NETTEST 40 x 1 MiB, removal mid-run,
  2 cycles: each re-initialized, 40 of 40 rounds verified (the round cut
  by the removal bridged by 5 TFTP retransmits). Shutdown halt clean
  ("halted: tx 56877/0"). **PASS.**

## Legs, RNDIS (configuration 2)

`CDC98R.INF`'s preset did not reach the existing instance keys (98's
network setup behaviour the earlier record describes), so the guests'
`Net\000n` keys were exported with `regedit /e`, and `Protocol="1"` was
imported with `regedit /s` from a second floppy folder swapped in over
QMP (`change floppy0`) before the device was plugged; the trace shows
"Protocol parameter: ... value 1" and configuration value 2. Same binary
as the ECM legs. NETTEST 12 x 1 MiB per cycle.

- **QEMU defect first** (`r1`, `r2`, NUSB EHCI): QEMU aborted right after
  RNDIS initialized ("usb_packet_copy: assertion failed"). Instrumented:
  an interrupt IN packet on endpoint 1 already held 64 bytes when 8 more
  were copied. `usb-net`'s RNDIS status endpoint copies its 8-byte
  notification before deciding to NAK; QEMU's EHCI retries the same
  packet after a NAK, so the bytes piled up until the assertion. Not a
  driver fault; fixed in the test patch (NAK before copying). The UHCI
  legs never hit it (UHCI sets each retry up afresh).
- **98 SE, NUSB 3.3 EHCI, High Speed** (`r3`): 2 removal cycles under
  traffic (cut in rounds 6 and 5, each bridged by 5 TFTP retransmits):
  both re-initialized as RNDIS at High Speed, 12 of 12 rounds verified
  each (6.4-10.2 s per MiB when not cut), the stale `OID_PNP_SET_POWER`
  refused after each halt. Shutdown halt clean ("halted: tx 14941/0").
  **PASS.**
- **98 SE, xHCI98 3.1.0.0, High Speed** (`w98 r1`): 2 cycles: both
  re-initialized as RNDIS, 12 of 12 rounds verified each (about 3 s per
  MiB). Cycle 1's removal came in round 9 (bridged by 5 retransmits);
  cycle 2's after NETTEST had finished. Shutdown halt clean. **PASS.**

ECM is faster than RNDIS on xHCI98 here and slower on NUSB's EHCI
(about 20 s against 7-9 s per MiB): timings under this host load are
noisy, and this is not a throughput result.

## Windows ME, xHCI98 3.1.0.0, ECM, High Speed

After the 98 SE legs. Overlay of `me-hcd310` (ME with xHCI98 3.1.0.0),
the same trace binary, `CDCT98.INF` on the floppy.

- Install (`m1`): plugging the device opened Add New Hardware with
  "RNDIS/QEMU USB Network Device"; the automatic search found
  `A:\CDCT98.INF`, copied `cdcnet.sys`, and the miniport initialized at
  once (ECM, configuration value 1, "high speed"). The restart ME asked
  for ran as a cold start (`reboot=shutdown`).
- `m2`: 2 cycles of NETTEST 12 x 1 MiB with removal under traffic (each
  in round 12, bridged by 5 TFTP retransmits): 21/21 ICMP and 12 of 12
  rounds verified each (3.2-3.7 s per MiB when not cut); both removals
  drained, halted and re-initialized at High Speed. Cycle 1 went through
  the path `2e6b56a` fixed ("device gone: 6 transfers outstanding",
  "drained"). **PASS.**
- Shutdown: unlike 98 SE, ME powered off **without halting** the
  miniport: the trace ends with "fault c0000001 usbd c0000005" and a
  pipe-reset recovery (the USB stack going down first), then QEMU exited
  on power-off, twice (`m1`, `m2`). Harmless here (no hang, nothing to
  save); noted for the ME standby/resume work.

## Not run here, and why

- **Windows ME**: RNDIS, and any leg under an EHCI driver: no ME image
  here has one (NUSB is not installed on any ME image in this kit).
- **NCM**: QEMU has no NCM model (as before).
- **Real hardware**: every leg here is QEMU; the High Speed `usb-net` is
  a test model, so the "Tested" cells still need a device on a PC.
- **`rtl815x`, `ax88179`**: no QEMU model; their 98 SE legs need the
  physical dongles. No repository script passes a physical device
  through to a guest, so those legs are owed.
