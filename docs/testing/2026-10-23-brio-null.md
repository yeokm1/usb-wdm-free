# The 98 SE audio-load wedge: no driver on the UAC 1.0 function, and uac2 counted, 2026-10-23

Question (owner, via xHCI98 43.60 part 1 and 36.41): is the Windows 98 SE
wedge at the plug of a USB audio device (Close Program: "Msgsrv32 [Not
responding]") in 98 SE's own UAC 1.0 class driver `usbaudio.sys`, or in
the KS / SysAudio / WDMAud layers every WDM audio driver shares? Test: an
INF that gives the UAC 1.0 function no driver, then the same plug cycles
with and without it. Development leg in QEMU, not a release and not
"Tested".

## The INF

`src/audio/noaud/NOAUD98.INF`: per-device hardware-ID lines for QEMU's
`usb-audio` (`USB\VID_46F4&PID_0002&MI_00`, `MI_01`) and the Logitech Brio
100's microphone (`USB\VID_046D&PID_094C&MI_02`, `MI_03`), class `USB`, an
install section with nothing in it (no `DevLoader`, no `NTMPDriver`; a
null service on NT). On 98 SE a hardware-ID match outranks
`wdma_usb.inf`'s class match: a new devnode installed from it silently, as
"QEMU USB Audio control (no driver)", driver key `InfPath=NOAUD98.INF`,
`InfSection=NoDriver`, no loader value (read from `regedit /e`).

What 98 SE makes of the devices (read from `HKLM\Enum\USB`): `usb-audio`
has one function devnode, `MI_00` (the control interface; the streaming
interface has none of its own); the Brio 100 under xHCI98 has `MI_00`
(video, `UVCM298.INF`) and `MI_02` (the microphone association,
interfaces 2 and 3). The `MI_01` and `MI_03` lines never match here and are
harmless.

## Rig

Kit `E:\usb-wdm-free-vm\brio-null\` (not tracked; `launch.ps1`, `rep.sh`,
`batch0.sh`, `batch1.sh`, `uaclaunch.ps1`, `x4cyc.sh`, `k.sh`). The
pinned `qemu-system-orphan.exe` (11.1.0 with the ISO patches), TCG, one
vCPU per guest, 256 MB, `-machine pc,smm=off -cpu pentium3`, headless, two
guests at a time, on a loaded host. Guests are copies of xHCI98 43.60's
bases (read-only): `b-qp` (xHCI98 on `qemu-xhci`, the Brio 100 and
`usb-audio` installed on `usbaudio.sys`) and `b-up` (98 SE's own
`uhcd.sys` on `piix3-usb-uhci`, `usb-audio` installed on `usbaudio.sys`).
The INF bases `ix` / `iu` are those with `NOAUD98.INF` copied to
`C:\WINDOWS\INF` and a new `usb-audio` instance (`serial=N2`) installed
from it; on `ix` the Brio's `MI_02` Enum key was deleted and the Brio
re-plugged, so its microphone too installed from the INF.

One cycle (`rep.sh`): restore a snapshot of a booted, idle desktop taken
on each base (`savevm desk`, the device absent), hot-add the device, read
at +50 and +100 s (screenshot, Ctrl+Esc, screenshot, Esc). **Wedge**: the
Start menu does not open at +100 s. Then four EIP samples, `info usb`, and
Ctrl+Alt+Del for the Close Program list. 43.60 cold-booted each
repetition; a snapshot restore is used here for time, the state restored
being the same booted desktop.

## Control: uac2 on the same KS stack

98 SE under xHCI98 (a copy of `xhci-legs\m98a`, `uac2wdm.inf` installed,
`USBAUDIO.SYS` renamed), stock QEMU, `qemu-xhci,p2=8,p3=0`, the uac2 build
the 2026-10-22 98 SE leg played with (`nusb-legs\w7\uac2wdm.sys`, 21,216
bytes) and `NOAUD98.INF` in `C:\WINDOWS\INF`; `uacemu.py` (X4 layout) over
`usb-redir`.

- First plug (HID/CDC wizards cancelled): the volume icon appears;
  `UACPLAY /playexit`: `waveOut devices=1`, 8 of 8 buffers, 96000 samples,
  `Close=0`, **PASS**; the simulator captured 385,752 bytes, 95,840
  non-silent frames.
- Ten unplug / replug cycles in the same boot (`x4cyc.sh`, the Start menu
  read at +60 s): the Start menu opened at every read, but the count is
  **not valid**: the first replug brought back a "UAC2 X4 layout" new
  hardware wizard (a function the first plug's wizard was cancelled for),
  and while it stood open 98 SE enumerated nothing more (the simulator
  logs of cycles 2 to 10 hold a connection and no control request). A
  play then failed (`completed=0`, the device not started).
- After the wizards were cancelled the driver started (volume icon), and
  `UACPLAY /playexit` **PASS** again: 8 of 8 buffers, 96000 samples,
  `Close=0`; the simulator captured 385,008 bytes.

So the control shows no regression to the KS stack with `NOAUD98.INF`
present (two plays). Those were two arrivals, not a rate; the count
follows.

## The counted uac2 arrival rate (2026-10-23, afternoon)

The same question for this repository's own driver: does a PortCls
driver arriving through KS / SysAudio / WDMAud wedge 98 SE as
`usbaudio.sys` does?

- **Image** `ux` (over `uc1`): a kit-only INF, `X4NODRV.INF` (not
  shipped), gives the simulated X4's HID (`MI_00`) and CDC (`MI_01`,
  `MI_02`) functions no driver, so no new-hardware wizard holds 98 SE's
  enumeration; `MI_03` stays on `uac2wdm.inf`. Its first plug installed
  them silently (registry read) and the volume icon appeared, no wedge.
  Snapshot `desk` of the booted idle desktop with the X4 absent (`sx`,
  copy `sx2` for the second guest).
- **One cycle** (`urep.sh`): restore `desk`, start `uacemu.py`, hot-add
  `usb-redir`, Start-menu reads at +50 and +100 s as above, EIPs, the
  Close Program list. Whether the driver came up is read from the tray's
  volume icon (`tray.py`: pixels differing from the desktop shot in the
  icon's box; 233 with the icon, 0 without).

| Run | Wedged | Driver up (volume icon) on the live ones |
|---|---|---|
| `ua01`-`ua07` (slot 0) | 4 of 7 (`ua02`, `ua04`, `ua05`, `ua06`) | 3 of 3 |
| `ub01`-`ub06` (slot 1) | 5 of 6 (all but `ub04`) | 1 of 1 |
| **uac2, total** | **9 of 13** | 4 of 4 |
| no device, same snapshots (`na`, `nb`) | 0 of 9 | - |

Every uac2 wedge has the same signature as `usbaudio.sys`'s: Close Program
lists "Msgsrv32 [Not responding]" (`shots\ua02-cad.png`), the EIP samples
are in the VMM (`C000A881`, `C000A4D8`, `C00036xx`, `C0051551`), and the
volume icon never appears. The snapshots themselves do not wedge: nine
restores with no device attached stayed live. Before the wedge the guest
read the X4's descriptors (ten control transfers in the simulator's log),
as in the live runs.

## Verdict

**The wedge is not `usbaudio.sys`'s own: it comes with the arrival of a
WDM audio device on 98 SE, whichever driver serves it.** With no function
driver on the UAC 1.0 function it did not occur in 30 cycles (16 of 30
without), but this repository's PortCls driver `uac2`, arriving through
the same KS / SysAudio / WDMAud path, wedged in 9 of 13 with the same
Close Program list and VMM addresses. The common part is what 98 SE does
when an audio function's driver starts (MSGSRV32 and the layers it
loads); `usbaudio.sys` is one way in, `uac2` another. It agrees with the
`usbmidi` leg on 98 SE's UHCI, where the guest froze on the first WDM
audio arrival also without `usbmidi` (`2026-10-22-usbmidi-m1.md`).

So: (i) `NOAUD98.INF` is a workable workaround for the Brio 100 on 98 SE
(the microphone is ignored, the video function keeps `uvc98`), because
with it no audio driver starts at all; (ii) a UAC 1.0 path in this
repository's own driver would **not** avoid the wedge as far as these
counts go: its premise fails in QEMU.

## Evening: the first arrival after a boot, and a boot-time workaround tried (2026-10-23)

### Reconciliation with uac2-rel's 0 of 12

The uac2-rel agent counted uac2 on 98 SE under xHCI98 with the same
method and read 0 wedges in 12 (`E:\usb-wdm-free\nusb-legs\ev\s1..s12`).
Its `desk` snapshot had been taken after one X4 arrival and removal in that
boot, so all 12 were later arrivals; from a snapshot taken straight after
boot (`deskF`, build 10) it then read **8 of 12** first arrivals wedged
against **0 of 12** later ones (`f1..f12-cyc.txt`). Every arrival counted
here was a first arrival after a boot: `sx` / `sx2` were saved from a fresh
boot in which the X4 had not been plugged. So the counts agree, and
neither the build nor the way the snapshot is restored explains the
difference; both were checked:

| Variable checked | Runs | First arrivals wedged |
|---|---|---|
| the 2026-10-22 build, a new QEMU process per cycle with `-loadvm` (above) | `ua`, `ub` | 9 of 13 |
| the same build, ONE QEMU process and `loadvm desk` from the monitor per cycle, as uac2-rel's `cyc.sh` | `ic1`-`ic10` | 4 of 10 |
| build 9 (uac2-rel's `nx\uac2wdm.sys`, SHA-256 `04615eaa...ba5d0`), new process per cycle | `9a01`-`9a10` | 5 of 10 |
| build 9, one process, monitor `loadvm` | `9i1`-`9i10` | 5 of 10 |
| uac2 under NUSB 3.3's EHCI (the uac2 leg's `nv1` copy, whole-device binding), a cold boot per cycle | `ca`, `cb` | 6 of 13 |

The NUSB runs show the same signature (Close Program "Msgsrv32 [Not
responding]", VMM EIPs) with the X4 on NUSB's EHCI, so the host
controller driver does not matter either. (A first NUSB attempt with
`-loadvm` is void: the stock QEMU asserted in `msix_vector_unuse` at the
restore and exited.)

The variable is **which arrival it is**: the first USB audio arrival after
a boot wedges in about half the cycles or more, whatever the driver
(`usbaudio.sys`, `uac2`, uac2-rel's `uac1` 4 of 9) and whatever the host
controller driver; once one arrival has come through, later ones do not
(uac2-rel's 0 of 12).

### Workaround tried: the audio stack up at boot

The owner asked (time-boxed) whether loading the KS audio stack at boot,
before any USB audio device arrives, avoids it, for instance with a
root-enumerated dummy WDM audio device. The strongest form of that needs
no new driver: a real WDM audio device present at boot. `bootrep.sh`: a
fresh overlay of `ux9` (build 9), a cold boot.

- **present**: the X4 simulator attached from QEMU's start, so `uac2`
  starts during boot with the rest of the system; the desktop read at boot
  +170 and +220 s; then unplug, replug (the first hot arrival of that
  boot), read at +50 and +100 s.
- **control**: no device at boot; the X4 hot-added at boot +170 s.

| Mode | Boot | The next hot arrival |
|---|---|---|
| present (`bp1`-`bp7`) | **7 of 7 live**, volume icon at the desktop (the driver up) | **5 of 7 wedged** (`bp1`, `bp3`-`bp6`) |
| control (`bq1`-`bq6`) | 6 of 6 live | 2 of 6 wedged (`bq2`, `bq3`); uac2-rel's 8 of 12 |

So **the stack being up at boot does not protect the next arrival**: with
`uac2` running from boot, the first hot arrival still wedged in 5 of 7,
with the same EIPs (`C000A881`, `C000A882`, `C00036xx`, `C0051551`). A
root-enumerated dummy would do no more than the X4 present at boot does, so
it was not built. What protects later arrivals is a hot arrival having
come through, not the stack being loaded. Not found: a way to make that
first hot arrival happen safely.

**Practical advice that the readings support**: a USB audio device
plugged in before Windows starts came up with Windows in 7 of 7 boots and
worked (volume icon); the freeze came when a device was plugged in, or
unplugged and plugged back, while Windows was running. So on 98 SE: plug
the USB audio device (or a webcam with a microphone) in before switching
on, and leave it plugged in until shutdown; if Windows freezes at a plug,
restart with the device plugged in. Whether starting Windows with the
device plugged in is safe on real hardware is not read (QEMU only).

## Not covered

- The Brio's video under the INF: the binding is unchanged (`MI_00` on
  `UVCM298.INF`) but no capture was read in the INF cycles.
- The `README.TXT` Update Driver route (replacing an installed
  `usbaudio.sys` devnode by the INF) was not run in a guest: the legs
  installed the INF on new devnodes.
- Where in the shared path the wait is (MSGSRV32, SysAudio, WDMAud): not
  read; no trace beyond EIP samples.
- Real hardware: all counts are QEMU TCG.
- 98 SE's NUSB EHCI: NUSB does not split the Brio (43.60), so the INF has
  nothing to match there.
