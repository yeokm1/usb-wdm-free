# uvc98 on Windows 2000 under xHCI98: "no picture" re-read, 2026-10-24

The README's evaluation note (d) says uvc98 shows no picture on Windows
2000 under xHCI98. The note comes from the 2026-10-22 leg
(`2026-10-22-98se-legs.md`, "uvc98 on 2000 SP4, xHCI98 3.1.0.0, Brio 100"):
160x120 failed with almost every packet empty, and the stop's alternate-0
select failed. This record reruns that leg to find whether uvc98 or xHCI98
is at fault, under a time box (owner, until the 0.1.0 cut). These are
development legs in QEMU, not "Tested".

  - the qemu-trace checked build, `xhci98.sys` 858C83B3...;

  - the gated record `out\gated-x86-qemu.txt`, commit b47a89f;
  - copied over 3.1.0.0 in the guest, then a clean restart.
- `uvcm2.sys`, the checked VM-trace build of rel-fixes 4511c85, with its

## Reading

- The 2026-10-22 2000 leg ran on the stock QEMU. The 98 SE legs, which
  passed, ran on the run-orphan QEMU. On the stock QEMU the Brio's stream
  is almost all empty packets on 2000, whatever the xHCI98 build. On the
  run-orphan QEMU, 2000 under xHCI98 batch 10 shows a picture at 160x120.
  So most of note (d) came from that difference, not from uvc98.
- The simulated camera (`usb-redir`, no Brio ids, no bandwidth quirk)
  passes every size on 2000 under xHCI98 3.1.0.0 and batch 10.
- The real Brio under xHCI98 on run-orphan still fails:
  - payloads come with the UVC error bit (bit 6 of the header): the camera
    reports its own stream errors;
  - every stop's SET_INTERFACE(alternate 0) ends in a STALL (xHCI98 trace:
    transfer event code 6, URB function 01 status 4);
  - 320x240 fails the same way under xHCI98 on 98 SE
    (`2026-10-22-98se-legs.md`).
  The same camera passes 160x120 to 320x240, with clean alternate-0
  selects, under Windows 2000's own EHCI driver on the same QEMU build
  (`2026-10-22-uvc-m2.md`).
- uvc98 runs the same USB code on 98 SE and 2000 (no NT-only path in
  `uvcusb.c`), and the same driver passes with the simulator. So the
  remaining failure is not in uvc98. It is in the real-camera path through
  QEMU's xHCI with `usb-host`, or in xHCI98's isochronous IN handling of a
  real device. These legs cannot tell which: the simulator does not
  reproduce it, and no real xHCI PC was available. Nothing in xHCI98 was
  changed.

- QEMU run-orphan 2EDD5707, TCG, `-cpu pentium3`, `qemu-xhci` only.

- the M1CAP reports beside them.

## Binding on 2000 (owner decision)

2000's own hub does not split the camera. The whole device carries its
first interface's compatible ids, so uvc98's `Class_0E&SubClass_01` line
binds the whole camera, and its microphone has no driver
(`docs/design/uvc.md` section 4). The
2000 UHCI leg of rel-fixes 4511c85 ran that way: `uvcsim.py` `FFFF:00E0`
at Full Speed, 160x120 PASS.
