# rtl815x on XP to 7: RTLXP7.INF and the x64 build, 2026-10-27

Task 44.70 part B3 (owner, 2026-10-27, xHCI98 3.3.0.0): `rtl815x` on
Windows XP, XP x64, Vista and 7, x86 and x64, for the chips Realtek's own
XP to 7 drivers do not list (`ethernet.md` section 7.6). Simulator legs
only: no such adapter exists here. Not a release, not "Tested".

## What was built

- `build.cmd ndis51` (new): WDK 7.1 WNET amd64, NDIS 5.1, cdcnet's flags.
  Import gate `imports-ndis51.txt`. `ndis5` unchanged.
- `netcore.c`: NDIS 5.1 registration under `NDIS51_MINIPORT` with a
  surprise-removal handler, as cdcnet. `netusb.c`: the trace port write on
  amd64, `NetUsbSurpriseRemoved`, and a completion that stays counted to
  its end (review finding, both identical copies, `ax88179` too).
- `RTLXP7.INF` 0.2.0.0, one models section per system:

## Rig

- QEMU 11.1.0, TCG, 4 vCPUs, `-usb` plus `usb-ehci`, Windows' own EHCI
  driver. Each guest a fresh overlay of a clean base in
  `E:\usb-wdm-free\vm\` (`wxp`, `wxp64`, `wv86`, `wv64`, `w786`, `w764`,
  hashes in that folder's `copy-sha256.txt`). Ports 49270 to 49275.
  Every start and stop is in the kit's `vmlog.txt`. Every guest stopped by
  QMP `quit`.
- The device: `rtlemu.py` (branch `rtl-sim`, merged) over usbredir at
  High Speed, on the EHCI port 1. Its SIMULATED host 192.168.77.1 answers
  DHCP, ARP and ICMP. `--flap-after 25 --flap-for 10` pulls the cable once.
- Install: XP and XP x64 through the Found New Hardware wizard from the
  floppy, Vista and 7 through `pnputil -a` in an elevated prompt.
- Signing on Vista x64 and 7 x64: a throwaway test certificate made in
  the guest (WDK 7.1 `MakeCert`), the image signed in the guest, test
  signing on, as the 2026-10-24 xpad98 leg did. The owner's certificate
  was not used. Nothing was signed on the host.
