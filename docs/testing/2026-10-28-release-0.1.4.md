# Release 0.1.4: newer Realtek chips in rtl815x, 2026-10-28

Owner, 2026-10-27 (xHCI98 3.3.0.0, task 44.70 part B): `rtl815x` serves
the Realtek RTL8153D, RTL8156, RTL8156B, RTL8157 and RTL8159. It runs on
98 SE, ME and 2000 for every chip. On XP to 7 it covers only the chips
that Realtek's driver in `usb-wdm-nonfree` does not. Branch `rtl-new`,
after `v0.1.3` (`062989b`). Three branches were merged into it:
`rtl-sim` (the simulator), `rtl-5g10g` (the RTL8157 and RTL8159) and
`rtl-pkg` (XP to 7 and x64).

Not "Tested": no adapter with any of these chips has run. Every leg is
with the simulator (`rtlemu.py`).

## What changed

- `rtl815x` 0.2.0.0 (`rtl815x.rc`, `DriverVer=10/27/2026,0.2.0.0`):
  - the chip core (`ethernet.md` sections 6.4.0 and 6.4.1);
  - `RTL81598.INF` (98 SE, ME) and `RTL815X.INF` (2000) add `0BDA:8155`,
    `8156`, `8157` and `815A`;
  - the new `RTLXP7.INF` for XP to 7 (`ethernet.md` section 7.6).
- `netcore.c` and `netusb.c` (the same in `ax88179`): NDIS 5.1
  registration for x64, a surprise-removal handler, and a completion that
  stays counted to its end. `ax88179`'s released image is 0.1.1's. The
  fix reaches it at its next release.
- Every other file is 0.1.3's.

## The release operation

`release\release.ps1 -Definition release\0.1.4.psd1 -NonFree
<usb-wdm-nonfree 418dda1>`: PASS, 89 files (0.1.3 had 85).

- `-Part x86` was not used. It leaves out every x64 folder, and so it
  would have removed the other packages' signed x64 images from `free/`.
- The two `rtl815x` x64 folders (`wxp-x64`, `wv-7-x64`) are commented out
  in `0.1.4.psd1`. They wait for the owner's test signature on the new
  x64 image. Then they are uncommented and the operation runs again with
  `-X64Dir`, before the tag.
- `STAGE.TXT`: `rtl815x` stays copy only on every system. Its relation
  to the non-free Realtek driver is EXCLUDES on 98 SE, ME, 2000 and 8 and
  later, and COEXISTS on XP to 7 (disjoint ids).

## Signing: a new test certificate (owner, 2026-10-28)

The owner lost the old test-signing key (thumbprint
`814CF778B45159EC2659C39C16C7E75EB3C37145`) and made a new self-signed
code-signing certificate, `CN=usb-wdm-free test signing`, thumbprint
`E6A2749B78E9E4074260CF79972A57EEB6E76C0A`, valid to 2036-10-28. Every x64 image of 0.1.4
is signed with the new key, so the release has one certificate.
`usb-wdm-free-test.cer` in the repository root is the new one.

- New images: `rtl815x` (`wxp-x64`, `wv-7-x64`) and `uac2` (`wxp-x64`,
  `wv-8-x64`), signed from the unsigned builds above (`bcdbe02d...` at
  `7f27537`, `1d84cc47...` at `e365a0d`).
- Unchanged images: `usbmidi` (`wxp-x64`, `wv-8-x64`), `xpad98`
  (`wxp-x64`, `wv-x64`) and `cdcnet` (`wxp-x64`, `wv-7-x64`). No unsigned
  build of them is at hand whose image digest matches. Each was signed
  again from the released image with its signature removed: the file cut
  at its certificate table, the security directory entry and the checksum
  zeroed. The Authenticode SHA-1 image digest skips exactly these bytes,
  so it is unchanged: `usbmidi` `B98962E9...`, `xpad98` `ADFDF726...`,
  `cdcnet` `05D2DAEF...`.
- `release.ps1` takes `strip:<path>` in `X64Builds` for these. It removes
  the signature of the image in the tree the same way and checks that
  the newly signed image differs from it only in the checksum, the
  security directory entry and the certificate table.
- `release\release.ps1 -Definition release\0.1.4.psd1 -X64Dir <signed>
  -Thumbprint E6A2749B78E9E4074260CF79972A57EEB6E76C0A -Certificate
  <usb-wdm-free-test.cer> -NonFree <usb-wdm-nonfree v1.1.0>`: `-Check`
  PASS, then PASS, 93 files (89 plus `rtl815x`'s two x64 folders of two
  files each). Every one of the ten x64 images read signed by
  `E6A2749B...`, no timestamp, and matched its unsigned form (the build,
  or the released image with its signature removed) outside the checksum
  and the certificate table. `STAGE.TXT` changes only in the two new
  `rtl815x` COPY lines and the notes of `uac2`'s two x64 COPY lines; no
  STAGE line changed.
