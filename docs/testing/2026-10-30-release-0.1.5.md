# Release 0.1.5: uac2 0.1.5.0 and usbcom 0.1.5.0, 2026-10-30

Owner, 2026-10-30: 0.1.4 plus `uac2` 0.1.5.0 (one wave and topology pair
per endpoint, the 9x Full Speed rule, the MEDIA class co-installer
`UACCI1.DLL` with `UACCI.INF`) and `usbcom` 0.1.5.0 (the chip modules).
Every other file is 0.1.4's. [Release notes](../release-notes.md),
section "Release 0.1.5". The legs are in the records the release notes
name; this record is the release operation.

## The build results

- `uac2` (`wdmx86 free`, `wdm64 free`, `uacco x86`, `uacco x64`) and
  `usbcom` (`wdmx86 free`, `vxd9x free`): built at `e421336`, clean,
  with `PASS.TXT`; the release ran at `aa6794a`, and the release
  operation's freshness check found no source change between the two.
- The x64 `uac2wdm.sys` and `UACCI1.DLL`: the owner's signed copies of
  the `e421336` builds, in `E:\usb-wdm-free-wt\sign015`.

## rtl815x: 0.1.4's bytes

`rtl815x` has no commit since `v0.1.4`. Built again at `aa6794a`
(`build.cmd ndis5 free` and `ndis51 free`: PASS), it differs from 0.1.4's
images only in the PE TimeDateStamp and the checksum (x86: 5 bytes at
`0xD0` to `0xD2` and `0x120`, `0x121`; x64: the same two fields, plus the
certificate table entry of the signed image). The TimeDateStamp is
inside the Authenticode image digest, so a fresh link cannot match
0.1.4's signature: the first `-Check` failed with "byte 0xD8 differs from
the build" for both x64 folders. So `0.1.5.psd1` takes `rtl815x` from
the tree, as `usbmidi`, `xpad98` and `cdcnet` already were: the x86
image and every INF as `tree:`, the two x64 images as `strip:` (the
released image with its signature removed, its digest unchanged,
`F9222F98...`). `rtl815x` in 0.1.5 is 0.1.4's files byte for byte, and
its relation to the non-free Realtek driver is the split already in the
tree: `EXCLUDES` on 98 SE, ME, 2000 and 8 and later, `COEXISTS` on XP to
7 x64.

## The release operation

`release\release.ps1 -Definition release\0.1.5.psd1 -X64Dir
E:\usb-wdm-free-wt\sign015 -Thumbprint
E6A2749B78E9E4074260CF79972A57EEB6E76C0A -Certificate
usb-wdm-free-test.cer -NonFree <usb-wdm-nonfree v1.1.0-2-g6a7243d>`
(its `STAGE.TXT` and `non-free/` are v1.1.0's; the two later commits
change its README only):

- `-Check`: "release: checks PASS; -Check, nothing published".
- The release: "release: PASS; free\, STAGE.TXT and SHA256.TXT written
  (101 files)" (0.1.4 had 93: `UACCI.INF` and `UACCI1.DLL` in each of
  `uac2`'s four 2000 to 8.1 folders).
- Every x64 image read signed by `E6A2749B...` (0.1.4's certificate), no
  timestamp, and matched its unsigned form outside the checksum and the
  certificate table: the `e421336` build for `uac2`, the released image
  with its signature removed for `rtl815x`, `usbmidi`, `xpad98` and
  `cdcnet`.
- `STAGE.TXT` changes in `uac2`'s and `usbcom`'s notes and the four new
  `UACCI.INF` COPY lines; no package's STAGE systems changed.
