# Release 0.1.8: uac2 0.1.8.26, released 2026-11-05

0.1.7 plus `uac2` 0.1.8.26 (build 26). It brings:
- the queue depth `Uac2QueueMs` and the real-time worker with its
  fairness rule (design record 19);
- the volume range topped at 0 dB, with `Uac2VolAboveZero` (20);
- each device named by its USB product string (21).

It also carries the `STAGE.TXT` lines the owner approved on 2026-11-04
after their legs: `uac2`, `cdcnet` and `usbflop` staged on XP x64 (as on
XP x86), and `ax88179` staged on 2000. Those come from the merged
`uwf-uac2xp64` branch: [xp64-legs](2026-11-04-xp64-legs.md) and
[ax88179-sim](2026-11-04-ax88179-sim.md).

[Release notes](../release-notes.md), section "Release 0.1.8". The legs
of the changes are in:
- [uac2-queue](2026-11-04-uac2-queue.md),
  [uac2-queue-9x2k](2026-11-04-uac2-queue-9x2k.md) and
  [uac2-queue-nt6x86](2026-11-04-uac2-queue-nt6x86.md);
- [uac2-gain](2026-11-05-uac2-gain.md);
- [uac2-names](2026-11-05-uac2-names.md).

- **Certificate**: the test certificate whose SHA-1 thumbprint is
  `E6A2749B78E9E4074260CF79972A57EEB6E76C0A` (0.1.4's,
  `usb-wdm-free-test.cer`). The 0.1.4 to 0.1.7 images are signed with

- **Signing command**: the earlier records do not hold the signing
  command line. With the WDK 7.1 SignTool and the certificate in the
  owner's store, the equivalent embedded signature, no timestamp, is

  uac2.wxp-x64.uac2wdm.sys uac2.wv-8-x64.uac2wdm.sys`. The release
  operation then checks each image's embedded signer and that its image
  digest matches the unsigned build.
- **The release operation** after signing, at this commit, with this
  checkout's `out\`: `release\release.ps1 -Definition release\0.1.8.psd1
  -X64Dir E:\usb-wdm-free-wt\x64-018 -Thumbprint
