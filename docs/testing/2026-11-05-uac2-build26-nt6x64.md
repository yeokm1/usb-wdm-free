# uac2 build 26 (0.1.8.26) on 64-bit XP, Vista, 7, 8 and 8.1: the update and a short check, 2026-11-05

This is the short check that build 26 owed on the 64-bit systems (see
`2026-11-04-uac2-queue-nt6x64.md`, "Owed"). On each system:

- the update from 0.1.6.0 (from build 24 on XP x64 and 7 x64), by
  Device Manager on Vista, 7, 8 and 8.1;
- the device's name as Windows shows it (build 26 names the device by
  its USB product string, design section 21);
- a play and a record;
- `Uac2UsbStats`.

The legs were run by workers uwf-audio64 (XP x64, 7 x64) and uwf-b26x64
(Vista, 8 and 8.1 x64). They are development legs in QEMU (TCG), not
"Tested". Raw logs, screenshots and scripts are in
`E:\xhci98-vm\p46\uwf-audio64\` and `E:\xhci98-vm\p46\uwf-b26x64\`
(`STATE.md` in each indexes them).

## Rig

- **Emulator:** the usb4-emu QEMU fork build `cb36497` (`usb-redir`,
  `usb-host`, isochronous real-time fix), 2 vCPUs, `-Tablet`,
  `qemu-xhci` on q35. Vista and 7 run with the q35 bases' pinned clock
  (2026-09-20); 8 and 8.1 run on the host's date.
- **The kit:** `uac2wdm.sys` `4d22c9f8...` and `uac2wdm.inf`
  `ccc7efb7...` (DriverVer `11/05/2026,0.1.8.26`), the x64 files of the
  0.1.8 release kit, unsigned.
  - Each guest signs the `.sys` itself with a throwaway certificate
    made in that guest (MakeCert, Root and TrustedPublisher), with
    `bcdedit /set testsigning on`.
  - Vista and 7 install the INF as it is (no catalog).
  - 8 and 8.1 use a copy of the INF with a `CatalogFile=uac2wdm.cat`
    line added (`111235a9...`), and a catalog made in the guest
    (MakeCat) and signed with the same certificate.
- **Base disk per system (Vista, 8, 8.1 x64):** an overlay of the
  shared q35 base, then:
  1. xHCI98's test certificate trusted, plus the throwaway certificate
     and test signing, as above.
  2. xHCI98 3.4.0.0 as released, `nt-x64`, installed with
     `INSTALL.BAT /console /yes /nopause /usb`. It exited with code 3
     (xhci98 3.4.0.0 started) on all three, and the guest was then
     cold-started.
  3. The co-installer `UACCI.INF` (DefaultInstall).
  4. `uac2` 0.1.6.0 as released (`wv-8-x64`), re-signed in the guest,
     added with `pnputil -a`. Vista asked "Windows can't verify the
     publisher of this driver software", answered "Install this driver
     software anyway". 8 and 8.1 asked nothing.
  5. At the first plug of the simulated CX31993: DriverVersion 0.1.6.0,
     `Uac2UsbStats` 57 words, and a 5 s play PASS.
  6. The disk was then made read-only. The update ran on an overlay of
     it.
  - Vista's base shows Found New Hardware windows at each start, for
    devices with no driver. They were answered "Ask me again later"
    before each step.
  - 7 x64's base is the one of the sibling record (0.1.6.0, then build
    24 by Let me pick).
- **Devices:**
  - The simulator `uacemu.py`, through `usb-redir` on `qemu-xhci` port
    1:
    - `--profile cx31993` (product string "UAC2 cx31993 - SIMULATED")
      for the update and the play.
    - `--profile hs24` for the record.
  - The host's Creative Sound Blaster X4 (`041E:3278`) and X1
    (`041E:3286`), bound to WinUSB on the host and passed through with
    QEMU `usb-host`. One VM owned them at a time.
- **Play and record:** UACWAV `play 2 16 48000 5` (and `play 2 24 48000
  5` on `hs24`), `rec 2 16 48000 5` on `hs24`, `rec 1 16 48000 5` on the
  X1. With several devices present, `UACWAV_NAME` picks the device by
  its name. UACWAV's `list` gives the WinMM name (`szPname`).

## The update

1. The new INF was added with `pnputil -a` while the CX31993 was
   attached, on 0.1.6.0.
   - Vista asked the publisher question, answered Install anyway. 8 and
     8.1 asked nothing.
   - Published names: Vista `oem6.inf`, 8 `oem4.inf`, 8.1 `oem4.inf`.
2. Device Manager > the device > Properties > Driver > Update Driver >
   Browse my computer for driver software > the kit folder > Next.
3. Where Windows refused, Update Driver > Browse > "Let me pick from a
   list of device drivers on my computer", then the 0.1.8.26 entry >
   Next.

| System | From | Update Driver > Browse | Let me pick | Prompt, restart | After |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| XP x64 SP2 | build 24 | (staged with `SetupCopyOEMInf`, `oem7.inf`; moved with a test tool that calls `UpdateDriverForPlugAndPlayDevices` with `INSTALLFLAG_FORCE`) | - | - | 0.1.8.26 |
| Vista x64 SP2 | 0.1.6.0 | "The best driver software for your device is already installed" | listed 0.1.6.0 [31/10/2026] and 0.1.8.26 [5/11/2026]; 0.1.8.26 > Next: "Windows has successfully updated your driver software" | none, no restart | 0.1.8.26, `oem6.inf` |
| 7 x64 SP1 | build 24 | "The best driver software for your device is already installed" | 0.1.8.26 > Next: success | not recorded | 0.1.8.26, `oem6.inf` |

- On Vista and 7, Browse kept the installed driver, as in the sibling
  record (7, build 24). On 8 and 8.1, which install through the signed
  catalog, Browse installed 0.1.8.26 at once. Why the two differ was not
  looked into.
- XP x64 has no Device Manager row: its leg moved the devices with the
  test tool, as in the XP x64 exhaustive record.
- On Vista, 8 and 8.1 the wizard's title changed to the device's new name, "UAC2 cx31993 -
  SIMULATED", by the time it showed the result. Its device line still
  read the INF's name, "UAC2 USB audio (experiment)".
- On Vista, 7, 8 and 8.1, devices attached after the update, the `hs24`
  simulator and the real X4 and X1, installed 0.1.8.26 at their first
  plug with no driver question (Vista's Found New Hardware windows at
  the X4 and X1 plug are below). On XP x64 the X4 and X1 had been
  installed before, and were moved to 0.1.8.26 with the test tool.
  The X4 matched by its own hardware id, `USB\VID_041E&PID_3278&MI_03`;
  the X1 and the simulators by the class id.

## The check

`Uac2UsbStats` (each driver key, after the plays): 94 words, with
QueueMs 32, DataTransfers 8, TransferUs 4000, RealtimeRead 1,
Realtime 1, **WorkerPriority 16, WorkerThrottles 0** on every system and
every device. `Uac2QueueMs` 0x20 and `Uac2Realtime` 1, both written by
the INF.

- `szPname` is WinMM's 31-character name: the Sound panel's endpoint
  name ("Headphones") with the device name, cut at 31 characters.
- The Sound panel shows the endpoint "Headphones" over the line "UAC2
  cx31993 - SIMULATED".
- On Vista and 7 a 5 s play completed in 3.5-4.4 s by UACWAV's own
  timing. The same happened with 0.1.6.0 on the base disks (Vista
  3245 ms; 7 3432 ms) and with build 24 on 7 (3588 ms); the 7 numbers
  are in uwf-audio64's `STATE.md`. The cause was not looked into: the
  guest's tick count was not compared with the host's time over a play.
  (Vista's clock in the taskbar kept to the host's within about a
  minute over 47 minutes.)
