# absptr: absolute pointers on 98 SE to 7 x64, 2026-11-06

The readings behind `docs/design/absptr.md` section 3 and the legs of its
section 12. Development legs in QEMU (TCG) with QEMU's own `usb-tablet`
and `usb-wacom-tablet` and the simulator `src/hid/absptr/absemu.py`; no
real tablet, touch screen or pen. Raw logs, screenshots and scripts are
in the kit `E:\xhci98-vm\a347\wdm2\` (`ev\`, `shots\`, `scripts\`,
`pids.txt`).

## Rig

- Emulator: the usb4-emu fork build `cb36497` (QEMU 11.1.2, with
  `usb-redir`), `qemu-system-x86_64.exe` SHA-256
  `3e7f6bf5b98fde98f56761c500e73fba62dd9a078b4d6c430546e59c95c4812d`.
  This host's QEMU 11.1.0 hung at 98 SE's boot logo on the q35 base and
  was not used.
- Guests: overlays of the shared clean q35 bases (98 SE `ab1d4d65...`,
  ME, 2000, XP SP3) with xHCI98 3.4.1.0 as published (`xhci98.sys`
  `f67f2bd2...`), installed with `INSTALL.BAT /console /yes /nopause`
  (exit 3, then a cold start; XP: exit 0 after its wizards), and the 98 SE
  + NUSB 3.3 guest of this repository's kit (pc machine, `usb-ehci`;
  `E:\xhci98-vm\p45\w45-serial\bases\w98-nusb-ehci.qcow2`). 1 vCPU (XP
  2), TCG.
- `PTRTEST.EXE` (`ptrtest.c`, SHA-256 `5955e645...`) in the guest writes
  every change of `GetCursorPos` and the button states (GetAsyncKeyState)
  to COM1.
- The leg script (kit `scripts\absleg.py`) moves the device to six
  points, fractions of its range: (0.10, 0.10), (0.50, 0.50), (0.90,
  0.20), (0.25, 0.75), (0.75, 0.90), (0.02, 0.98) (for touch taps the
  last is (0.02, 0.85), off the taskbar); a point passes when the cursor
  is within 1 % of the screen of where it belongs. Then each button is
  held for 1 s and released. QEMU's own devices are driven with QMP
  `input-send-event`, the simulator over its control port.
- `profleg.sh` plugs a simulator profile (`usb-redir`), answers the
  hardware wizard, waits until the guest polls the interrupt endpoint, and
  runs the leg script.

### How each system installed it

- 98 SE: at the device's first plug the wizard's floppy search offered
  absptr over 98's own HID driver; later plugs of other listed devices:
  the same wizard, no restart.
- ME: the first plug installed ME's own HID driver with no wizard. Device
  Manager, the device's Properties, Update Driver, "Automatic search for
  a better driver" found `A:\ABSPTR.INF` and installed absptr, no
  restart. The touch screen, multi-touch and pen simulators plugged later
  got absptr at their first plug with no wizard (the INF was by then in
  ME's INF folder).
- 2000: a listed device not seen before got absptr at its first plug,
  after "Digital Signature Not Found" (Yes). The simulators that this
  2000 overlay had seen in the readings kept Windows' HID driver; Device
  Manager, Uninstall, "Scan for hardware changes" found them again with
  absptr.
- XP: every device first got XP's own signed "USB Human Interface
  Device" driver. Device Manager, Update Driver, "No, not this time",
  "Install from a list or specific location", "Don't search. I will
  choose the driver to install": the list showed "USB Human Interface
  Device" (signed) and absptr's entry; absptr's entry, "Continue Anyway",
  no restart. The PenPartner and `kvm16` then worked. The touch screen
  and pen did not move the pointer: their child device was still
  "HID-compliant device" (it kept the driver XP gave it for the digitizer
  collection). Uninstalling that child in Device Manager and "Scan for
  hardware changes" made it "HID-compliant mouse", and then the legs
  passed.

### Open points (both fixed, see "Legs with the fixes")

- **98 SE: the first touch after the device starts.** In all 7 leg
  script runs of `touch` and `mtouch` on 98 SE with this build (both host
  controller drivers, each on a fresh plug), the first tap moved the
  pointer to its point but its press was not seen; every later tap of the
  run was right, and a second run on the same plug was 6/6. By hand (the
  same simulator command on a fresh plug, 40 s or more after it), the
  first press was lost in 1 of 5 tries, once with the pointer already at
  the point. Not seen on ME, 2000 or XP. absptr queues the move and then
  the press (design section 6), and the press was sent by the simulator
  each time (its log); where it is lost is not read yet.
- **2000: "Unsafe Removal of Device"** at every unplug of an absptr
  device (screenshot `ev\final\fE2-w2k-unsafe-removal.png`); the
  driver does not set `SurpriseRemovalOK`.

### Outside absptr

- On 98 SE under xHCI98, QEMU's `usb-tablet` plugged into port 1 right
  after the `usb-wacom-tablet` was unplugged from that port never
  enumerated (no `Enum\USB` entry); on port 2 it did, and worked. Left to

- A development run on 98 SE + NUSB (the trace build, after many plugs)

## The first touch on 98 SE: what was read

The trace build (debug port lines, kit `g\n\e9-d3.log`) shows absptr
completing both reports of the first touch to hidclass, the move with
the old buttons and then the press, one read after the other, and the
press reaching hidclass; the press is lost above absptr, in Windows'
own HID mouse path, which is closed and not read. By hand the first
press of a fresh plug was lost now and then, also with the pointer
already at the point. The fix: after the first report hidclass reads
since a start, no report is read for 100 ms (design section 6). With it,
on a fresh 98 SE xHCI98 overlay, all 8 leg runs on fresh plugs (5
`touch`, 3 `mtouch`) passed their first tap (kit `ev\gx-run.log`).
