# The SHA-1 test certificate, 2026-11-06

xHCI98 roadmap 47.27 (owner, 2026-11-06). The x64 legs of the SR9800
and SR9700 drivers (`docs/testing/2026-11-06-sr98xx-sim.md` on branch
`w47-wdm4`, `b141b47`) found that 7 x64 refuses images signed with 0.1.4's test
certificate unless Microsoft's SHA-2 code-signing update is installed.
That certificate is itself signed with SHA-256. The owner made a new
one, signed with SHA-1, for every x64 image of the next release.

0.1.4's certificate (`E6A2749B78E9E4074260CF79972A57EEB6E76C0A`,
`CN=usb-wdm-free test signing`) reads sha256RSA. It stays in the root
until the release operation replaces it, and in every tag from 0.1.4 to
0.1.8.

## release.ps1

- `-Thumbprint` defaults to `7A3C6E62...`.
- A signer certificate, in each x64 image and in `-Certificate`, must be
  signed with SHA-1 with RSA (`1.2.840.113549.1.1.5` or the OIW
  `1.3.14.3.2.29`).
- `-Check` against 0.1.8's definition, with 0.1.8's twelve x64 images
  as `-X64Dir` and `-Thumbprint E6A2749B...`: each of the twelve and the
  certificate failed "signed with sha256RSA, not SHA-1 with RSA" (the
  other failures of that run were stale builds under `out\`).
- `-Check -Part x86 -Certificate release\usb-wdm-free-test-sha1.cer`
  with the default thumbprint: no certificate failure, and the
  certificate staged as `usb-wdm-free-test.cer`.
- Without `-Certificate`, `-Check` of the x64 folders with the
  default thumbprint failed on the root `usb-wdm-free-test.cer`
  (still 0.1.4's): "thumbprint E6A2749B..., not 7A3C6E62..." and
  "itself signed with sha256RSA".

## Legs

Rig: stock QEMU 11.1.0, q35, TCG; overlays of the project's q35 bases
(`w7x64` and `vx64` two vCPUs, `xpx64` one), each with xHCI98 3.4.1.0
(`nt-x64`) installed from its package; the simulators `ax7emu.py --chip
sr9800` (High Speed) and `sremu.py` (Full Speed, the SR9700) of
`w47-wdm4` over usb-redir on xHCI98's port 1; the INFs of `f45732b`
(`AX772F.INF` `96ad5050...`, `SR9700F.INF` `e1479e78...`) with the two
images above. On 7 x64 and Vista x64 the new certificate went into Root
and TrustedPublisher (`certutil -addstore`) and `bcdedit /set
testsigning on`, then a restart, then `pnputil -a` for both INFs. Per
chip: install, `sc query`, a DHCP lease from the simulated host
(192.168.77.2), `ping -n 4` to 192.168.77.1 at 32, 1472 and 4000 bytes,
removal under `ping -t`, replug with the same `ping -t` running.

**PASS on all three.** The 7 x64 guest carried none of KB4474419,
KB4490628 or KB3033929 (`wmic qfe`), the case that refused the images
signed with 0.1.4's certificate (event 3004, problem 0x34) on
`w47-wdm4`. The guests' clock was the bases' 2026-09-20, inside the new
certificate's validity. Vista x64 is the first leg of these images
there.
