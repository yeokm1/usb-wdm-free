# uac2 0.1.9 and the ALC4080 replay: 98 SE, 7 x64 and Vista x64, 2026-11-09

The open item "47.18 Realtek ALC4080 through `uac2`" ([open
items](open-items.md)) left, besides the real board: 98 SE under NUSB and
xHCI98, 7 x64, two streams at once, and the topology names on Vista and 7.
These legs run the owner's capture of a board's ALC4080 replayed
(`uacemu.py --profile alc4080cap`, on 98 SE with `--vid 0db0 --pid a073`,
on Vista and 7 with the capture's own id (uac2.md 15.1a), as the NT
matrix ran it: playback on
interfaces 4 to 6 to Output Terminals 21 speaker `0x0301`, 22 headphones
`0x0302` and 24 S/PDIF `0x0605`; capture on interfaces 1 to 3 from Input
Terminals 9 analog connector `0x0601`, 11 line connector `0x0603` and 12
microphone `0x0201`) with `uac2` 0.1.9 as released, on every output and
input, two streams at once, and the endpoint names Vista and 7 give.
Development legs in QEMU (TCG) with the simulator, not a release and not
"Tested"; the real board stays owed.

**Outcome.** 98 SE under both stacks and 7 x64 and Vista x64 under both:
every output played and every input Windows offers recorded the
simulator's tone; two streams at once (two outputs; an output and an
input) passed, the overlap read in the simulator's log (on 98 SE under
xHCI98 one of the two output-and-input cases did not overlap; the other
did). Vista and 7 name the outputs Speakers, Headphones and
Speakers (the S/PDIF output, by design: [uac2.md](../design/uac2.md),
the playback bridge is a speaker or headphones), the inputs Microphone
and Line; the analog connector input is an endpoint "Analog Connector"
that Vista and 7 keep out of WinMM (`DeviceState` `0x10000001`), as the
earlier legs read (uac2.md, the 7 SP1 x64 and Vista SP2 x64 rows) and as
Windows 10's own driver lists the same two inputs
([inbox-reference](2026-11-06-inbox-reference.md)).

## Rig

- **Driver:** `uac2` 0.1.9 as released: 98 SE `free/audio/uac2/w98se-me`
  (`uac2wdm.sys` `b3c4d555...`); x64 `free/audio/uac2/wv-8-x64`
  (`uac2wdm.sys` `0a3c02db98fd429b...`, the owner's signature, its
  root certificate imported in the guest).
- **Emulator:** the usb4-emu fork `cb36497` (`3e7f6bf5...`), one vCPU.
- **98 SE** (`pc` machine): the 9x matrix's bases `w98x` (NUSB 3.3 and
  xHCI98 3.4.2.0 `3990e17d`; the device on `qemu-xhci`) and `w98n2`
  (NUSB 3.3; the device on its EHCI), the INF staged on `DevicePath`.
- **7 x64, Vista x64** (q35): the 0.1.9 NT matrix's bases `w7x64-xh`
  (`d067346f...`), `vx64-xh` (`a4e4c4ad...`) with xHCI98 3.4.2.0, and
  `w7x64-win` (`d647972f...`), `vx64-win` (`60ede21c...`) with Windows'
  own EHCI; `UACCI.INF` and the INF staged as in that matrix.
- **Steps:** `UACWAV list`; `play` 2 channels 16-bit 48 kHz and `rec` 2
  channels on each pair in turn (`UACWAV_PAIR` 0 to 2); two at once: a
  play of 8 s started in the background, and in front a 5 s play on
  another output or a 5 s recording; on Vista and 7 the `MMDevices`
  registry (each endpoint's state and name). The overlap of the two
  streams is read in the simulator's log (both interfaces streaming).

- The legs also asked for a third input pair and a third two-at-once
  case with it; Vista and 7 offer two inputs, so those steps reported
  "no UAC2 input": the test's error, not a FAIL of the device.
- WinMM's order of the outputs differs between starts (Vista's restart
  listed Speakers, Headphones, Speakers); the names do not.
- Endpoints (`MMDevices`), all four legs: render Speakers, Headphones,
  Speakers, each `DeviceState` 1; capture Microphone and Line,
  `DeviceState` 1, and Analog Connector `0x10000001`. The device part of
  every endpoint's name differs by stack: "ALC4080 capture - SIMULATED"
  under xHCI98, "ALC4080 audio function - SIMULA" (cut at 31 characters)
  under Windows' EHCI, where the composite device is split and the audio
  function named on its own: the name each stack gives the function, not
  `uac2`'s.
