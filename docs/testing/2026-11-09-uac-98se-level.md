# The 98 SE level offset: where `uac2` and `uac1` streams lose 5.5 dB, 2026-11-09

On 98 SE every `uac1` and `uac2` stream reached the simulated device
about 5.5 dB below what `UACWAV` plays: -25.5 dBFS for its -20 dBFS sine
([uac1-formats](2026-11-09-uac1-formats.md)), and in the volume legs
-11.52 dBFS on 98 SE where XP read -6.02 for the half-scale ramp
([uac2-volume](2026-10-28-uac2-volume.md)). XP reads the level
correctly. Owner, 2026-11-09: find where 98 SE loses it; the 98 SE pass
mark is now "within 1 dB of the PCM control". Development legs in QEMU
(TCG) with the simulators, not a release and not "Tested".

## Rig

- **Emulator:** the usb4-emu fork `cb36497` (`3e7f6bf5...`), `pc`
  machine, one vCPU, 256 MB, `qemu-xhci`, `usb-ehci` and
  `piix3-usb-uhci`.
- **Base:** the `uac1` kit's `w98k` (98 SE with xHCI98), a fresh overlay
  per leg, the device on `qemu-xhci`.
- **Device and driver:** `uacemu.py --profile hs24` (High Speed, 24-bit,
  a Feature Unit with master and channel volume and mute) under `uac2`
  0.1.10.0 as released (`uac2wdm.sys` `b3c4d555...c0b9`, `free/` at
  `2fea99e`). The control: `uac1emu.py --profile headset` under Windows
  98 SE's own `usbaudio.sys` (nothing staged).
- **Tool:** `UACWAV.EXE` `1cc0f1b7...` (`8c1eb43`): `mix` now lists the
  source lines of each destination with their controls, and `srcvol
  <name|*> <v>` sets the volume of the speaker line's sources.
- **Reading:** the simulator's peak meter, every 0.25 s, of the samples
  as received (before the device's own volume, so a level the driver or
  Windows makes shows there) and as rendered through the device's units.

## Results (leg `uac2-w98-xh-lv1`)

The mixer as 98 SE set it up for the device (`UACWAV mix`):

| Line | Control | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Speaker (destination) | Master Volume (the driver's volume node), 96 steps | 65535, 65535 |
| Speaker | Master Mute | 0 |
| source Wave | Volume, 192 steps | **32845, 32845** |
| source Wave | Mute | 0 |
| source SW Synth | Volume, 192 steps | 32845, 32845 |
| destination "Unknown" (recording) | source Microphone, no controls | - |

The Speaker line's volume is the driver's topology node; the Wave and SW
Synth lines are not in the driver's topology: 98 SE adds them (for
Windows' own driver too, below) and applies their volume before the
samples reach the driver's pin.

| Step | What ran | Received (dBFS) |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | `play 2 16 48000 3`, the mixer as found | -25.5 |
| L2 | `vol 65535` (the speaker line: no change, it was at the top), play | -25.5 |
| L3 | `srcvol * 65535` (Wave and SW Synth to the top), play at 48 kHz | **-20.0** |
| L4 | the same, play at 44.1 kHz | -20.0 |
| L5 | the same, play mono at 48 kHz | -20.0 |

The 5.5 dB is the Wave line's default of 32845 (about half the slider),
applied before the samples reach the driver; with the Wave line at the
top every stream reaches the device at the level `UACWAV` plays, at both
rates and in mono and stereo. The driver's own gain is unity throughout
(the speaker node at 0 dB, the device's Feature Unit doing the volume:
rendered equals received). It is not the topology node's default or its
dB mapping (the node stood at its top in L1 and L2), not a format
conversion in the shared wave path (L3 to L5 exact), and not a level
98 SE writes for the device at install (none in the device's driver key).

## The control: Windows' own driver (leg `usbaudio-w98-xh-lv2`)

The same base with `uac1emu.py --profile headset` bound by 98 SE's own
`usbaudio.sys` ("USB Audio Device"), nothing of this repository
installed. Its mixer, as 98 SE set it up: the Speaker line's Volume (the
device's Feature Unit, 30 steps) at 43690 and Mute; the sources Wave,
SW Synth and CD Player each with a Volume at **32845** (192 steps), and
Microphone. The Wave line's default is the same under Windows' own
driver: on this base, for both devices read, it is 98 SE's, not one this
repository's drivers set (no other device or installation was read). Its plays did not open (`waveOutOpen` 8,
`MMSYSERR_NOTSUPPORTED`, at 48 and 44.1 kHz, stereo and mono), so no
level was read under it; that refusal is Windows' driver with this
simulated device, not looked into here.

## What follows

- Nothing in `uac2` or `uac1` changes: the offset is 98 SE's own default
  for its Wave line. A 98 SE user who finds a device quiet raises "Wave"
  in Volume Control (whether 98 SE keeps it across a restart was not
  read here).
- The level readings of the earlier 98 SE records stand as readings with
  the Wave line at 98 SE's default: -25.5 dBFS for -20, -11.52 for -6.02.
  A level criterion on 98 SE reads against the PCM control (owner,
  2026-11-09), or sets the Wave line to the top first (`UACWAV srcvol
  wave 65535`).
