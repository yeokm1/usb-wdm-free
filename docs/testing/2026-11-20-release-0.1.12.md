# Release 0.1.12: rtl815x 0.2.4.0 and cdcnet's INFs, prepared 2026-11-20

0.1.11 plus the network changes the owner chose for it (owner,
2026-11-20: "Network only"): `rtl815x`'s forced speeds, pause and
receive checksum choices and Advanced-tab entries (design
[ethernet.md](../design/ethernet.md) sections 4.10.4 and 4.10.9), and
`cdcnet`'s INFs. Every other package, the audio drivers included, is
0.1.11's, source and files. This record is the preparation and the
release operation's checks.
[Release notes](../release-notes.md), section "Release 0.1.12".

## What the release carries

- `src/net/rtl815x` as developed after 0.1.11, with the version raised:
  the forced speed and duplex written after the PHY reset has cleared
  (4.10.4), `*FlowControl` 0, 1 and 3, receive checksums on the RTL8157
  and RTL8159 (off by default), `EEEMaxSupportSpeed` on the 1 Gb/s
  class, no Advanced EEE on the RTL8159 (4.10.9).
- `src/net/cdcnet`'s INFs: the Advanced tab's `Protocol` value written
  only where none is stored; the test INFs regenerated by `gentest.py`.
  No code of `cdcnet` changed.
- The network design and records of that work: ethernet.md, and the
  records [advanced-tab](2026-11-09-advanced-tab.md) (its later notes),
  [advanced-tab-eeemax](2026-11-17-advanced-tab-eeemax.md),
  [netopts-legs](2026-11-17-netopts-legs.md),
  [netopts-rtl8157](2026-11-17-netopts-rtl8157.md),
  [rtl8157-forced-real](2026-11-17-rtl8157-forced-real.md),
  [rtl8157-latency](2026-11-17-rtl8157-latency.md),
  [trim-step0](2026-11-17-trim-step0.md),
  [rtl815x-sim-legs](2026-11-18-rtl815x-sim-legs.md), and open-items'
  network sections.
- Not carried (owner, 2026-11-20): the audio work after 0.1.11 (`uac2`,
  `uac1`, `src/audio/common`, their design and records), the MTP work,
  and the shared network core's changes in `ax88179`, `ax88772` and
  `sr9700`: those three keep 0.1.11's source and images.

`rtl815x`'s copy of the network core (`netcore.c`, `netchip.h`,
`shim/fakeos.*`, `coretest.c`) is therefore newer than the copies in
`ax88179`, `ax88772` and `sr9700` (ethernet.md 4.10.9, the note of
2026-11-20). No build, gate or test of the repository compares the
copies: each package's `build.cmd` and `test.cmd` compile only its own
folder.

unsigned build outside the checksum and the certificate table. The other

## Legs

The `rtl815x` source of this release, apart from the version lines, is
the source these records ran (`4c9ef60` and `65dd131`, whose
`src/net/rtl815x` equals it but for those lines):

- simulated adapters ([rtl815x-sim-legs](2026-11-18-rtl815x-sim-legs.md)):
  the forced speeds on XP SP3 under Windows' EHCI and on 98 SE under
  NUSB's EHCI and under xHCI98 3.5.0.0 (RTL8157, RTL8156B); 2000 SP4's
  options under its own EHCI; XP SP3's receive checksums under xHCI98;
- a real RTL8157 ([rtl8157-forced-real](2026-11-17-rtl8157-forced-real.md)):
  the forced values on XP SP3 under xHCI98 3.5.0.0 and on 98 SE under
  xHCI98 3.1.0.0.

Earlier states of the same work ran the records
[netopts-legs](2026-11-17-netopts-legs.md) and
[advanced-tab-eeemax](2026-11-17-advanced-tab-eeemax.md) (simulators).
The legs on this release's source (rtl815x-sim-legs,
rtl8157-forced-real) ran x86 `ndis5 trace` builds (the free build
writing a trace to QEMU's port 0xE9, test-only), the checksum probes a
scratch trace build with one more trace line; of the earlier states,
netopts-legs' second round (`d89f912`) ran its 98 SE pause legs with the
free build, and a 98 SE update control ran 0.1.10's released package. No
leg ran an x64 image. The released images are the free builds of this
release's source with only the version lines changed; they were not run
in a guest (owner, 2026-11-20). No guest leg ran for `cdcnet`'s INF
change. What open-items lists for `rtl815x` stays open (7 x64, the real
chip on 7, a matched forced full-duplex link, 98 SE's slow stream at
forced speeds on the real chip).
